# Recruiting Roadmap

## Preseason
- build or refresh your player profile
- collect baseline clips
- identify target programs

## Early season
- update your stats
- send first outreach wave
- start tracker follow-ups

## Midseason
- refresh reel quality
- add current clips
- continue professional follow-up

## Late season / playoffs
- tighten your materials
- target realistic opportunities
- prepare for tryouts, camps, and spring contact

## Offseason
- reassess goals
- rebuild clip bank
- update profile and bio for next season
