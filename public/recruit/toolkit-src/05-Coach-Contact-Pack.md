# Coach Contact Pack

## 1. Initial intro
Subject: Interested Player — [Name] ([Birth Year] — [Position])

Hello Coach,

My name is [Name]. I am a [Birth Year] [Position] from [Hometown] currently playing for [Team / League].

I wanted to introduce myself and share my player profile and highlight reel for your review. I am interested in learning more about your program and what you value in your recruiting process.

Thank you for your time.

[Name]
[Phone]
[Email]
[Profile link]
[Reel link]

## 2. Pre-tryout inquiry
Ask if tryout information or upcoming opportunities are available.

## 3. Highlight reel submission
Keep it short. Share the reel and one reason you fit their level.

## 4. Follow-up after no response
Send one polite follow-up 7–10 days later.

## 5. Post-showcase follow-up
Mention event, performance context, and include your materials.

## Avoid
- giant paragraphs
- vague messages
- attaching random files without context
- repeated spam follow-ups
