# POWR Recruit — Toolkit Content Specification

## 1. START HERE Guide
1–3 pages.

Include:
- welcome
- what POWR Recruit is
- recommended order of use
- 30–60 minute setup plan
- how to keep materials updated
- reminder that recruiting support does not replace development

## 2. Player Resume Builder
Editable template.

Fields:
- full name
- photo
- position
- shoots/catches
- birth year / DOB
- height / weight
- hometown
- current team
- league
- jersey number
- stats
- hockey history
- strengths / style
- academics
- intended program
- coach references
- contact info
- parent contact info
- highlight reel link

Include:
- blank template
- completed example
- quick “what good looks like” page

## 3. Player Bio Builder
Include prompts:
- What kind of player are you?
- What are your strongest traits?
- What do coaches rely on you for?
- What are you working to improve?
- What are your hockey goals?
- What off-ice qualities matter?

Formula:
1. Intro
2. Play style
3. Strengths
4. Character
5. Goals

Include sample bios for:
- forward
- defenseman
- goalie

## 4. Highlight Reel Blueprint
Sections:
- what coaches need quickly
- title card structure
- player identification
- best clips first
- clip variety
- recommended sequence
- skater clip categories
- goalie clip categories
- common mistakes
- final export checklist

## 5. Coach Contact Pack
Templates:
1. Initial intro
2. Pre-tryout inquiry
3. Highlight reel submission
4. Follow-up after no response
5. Post-showcase follow-up
6. Roster opportunity inquiry
7. Thank-you after skate/meeting
8. Parent-assisted version

For each:
- subject line
- message body
- what to customize
- what to avoid
