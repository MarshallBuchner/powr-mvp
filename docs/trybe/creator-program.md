## Creator brief

**Concept:** “I uploaded my skating to an AI hockey coach. Here's what it told me.”

Make this feel like a real hockey player or coach trying a useful tool. Speak in your own voice. Show what actually happened and what you found useful, including honest limitations. Avoid a scripted corporate-commercial delivery.

### Story sequence

1. Open with your own skating footage and the concept in your own words.
2. Show uploading that clip to POWR at trainwithpowr.com.
3. Show the real movement/skeleton analysis when it appears. Do not fabricate an overlay or imply it is a clinical measurement.
4. Show the resulting assessment/report, hiding account information and other people's personal information.
5. Pick one specific development takeaway from the actual report. Explain what you would focus on in your next practice, without promising a result.
6. Close with: “Try your first assessment free at trainwithpowr.com.” If mentioning the paid pack, say “5 assessments for CA$19 — a one-time purchase.” Use the approved trackable link supplied for your content once attribution is tested.

### Creative direction

- Lead with skating and your reaction, not a logo animation.
- Use natural narration, readable captions and close screen recordings.
- Keep the report legible long enough to understand the takeaway.
- Submit a clean original video and a captioned version if available. Keep important elements away from platform interface areas.
- Use footage, audio and music you have permission to use for the intended placements. Paid-ad rights and any creator-account permissions must be agreed before advertising.
- Clearly disclose the paid/commission relationship in the content and caption using the platform's appropriate disclosure tools. Do not present a compensated recommendation as independent.

### Claims and exclusions

POWR provides AI-assisted estimates for development and education. Feedback depends on camera angle, lighting and clip quality. Be honest about your experience.

Do not make or imply medical diagnoses, treatment, injury prevention, clinical accuracy, scouting grades, recruitment outcomes, guaranteed improvement, guaranteed speed/power gains or other unsupported performance claims. Do not invent testimonials, results or before/after improvements. Do not imply an AI estimate replaces a qualified coach. Describe the skeleton view as a visualization, not validated biomechanics.

Only show people and footage you have permission to include. Do not expose private account information or another player's report.
