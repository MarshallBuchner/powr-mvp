# Highlight Reel Blueprint

## What coaches need quickly
- who you are
- what position you play
- what level you play at
- how to identify you on the ice
- your best clips first

## Recommended structure
1. Title card (name, birth year, position, team, contact, jersey color/number)
2. Best clips first
3. Small variety of offensive examples
4. Small variety of defensive details
5. Transition / skating examples
6. Special teams if relevant
7. Final contact card

## Clip categories for skaters
- offensive zone play
- playmaking
- skating and transitions
- puck retrievals
- pressure / forecheck
- net-front habits
- special teams

## Common mistakes
- long intros
- low-quality clips
- too many average plays
- no player identifier
- random order

## Final export checklist
- exported in clear quality
- your name visible
- best clips first
- under a coach-friendly runtime
