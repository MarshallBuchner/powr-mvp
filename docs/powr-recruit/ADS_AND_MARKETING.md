# POWR Recruit — Ads & Marketing

## Strategy
Do not lead with “digital product”.

Lead with:
- parent confusion
- player frustration
- poor organization
- random clips/messages
- desire to look professional

The ad should make the viewer think:
“That is exactly what we're dealing with.”

# CREATIVE RULES

Use:
- 4:5 for Meta feed
- 9:16 for Stories/Reels/TikTok
- 1:1 optionally
- 1 message per ad
- 6–12 words max for main on-image hook
- price visible on product-stack ads
- mobile legibility first

Avoid:
- tiny paragraphs
- too many product cards at once
- guaranteed outcome claims
- fake coach quotes
- fake “trusted by 10,000 players”
