# POWR Recruit — START HERE

Welcome to POWR Recruit.

This toolkit is designed to help hockey players and families present themselves professionally, organize coach outreach, and track opportunities with more clarity.

## Recommended order of use
1. Complete your Player Resume Builder.
2. Draft your Player Bio.
3. Organize your highlight reel using the Blueprint.
4. Prepare your coach outreach with the Contact Pack.
5. Build your Recruiting Tracker.
6. Use the Tryout & Showcase Checklist before key events.
7. Review the Recruiting Roadmap throughout the season.
8. Compare your work against the Example Pack.

## 30–60 minute setup plan
- 10 min: collect your basics (stats, team history, contact info)
- 10 min: draft your player bio
- 10 min: list your best clips and build your reel order
- 10 min: set up your recruiting tracker
- 10–20 min: customize your first outreach templates

## What POWR Recruit is for
- presenting your information clearly
- making it easier for coaches to review you
- reducing chaos during recruiting
- helping families follow a process

## What POWR Recruit is not
- a guarantee of recruitment
- a promise of a scholarship or roster spot
- a replacement for on-ice development

## Keep your materials current
Update your profile, stats, and reel every time your situation changes.
