# POWR Recruit — Product Spec

## Product
POWR Recruit

## Descriptor
The Complete Hockey Recruiting Toolkit

## Price
$39 CAD one-time

## Target buyer
Primary:
- hockey parents
- players ages ~14–21
- AA/AAA/junior/prep/college hopefuls
- players looking for teams or opportunities

Secondary:
- coaches helping players prepare materials
- advisors or trainers assisting player families

## Problem
Recruiting is fragmented:
- clips live in random folders
- stats are scattered
- coach contact is inconsistent
- players don't know what to send
- parents don't know how to organize the process
- follow-ups are easy to forget
- highlight reels often lack structure

## Transformation
Before:
- random clips
- weak emails
- scattered stats
- poor tracking
- confusion

After:
- polished player profile
- structured highlight reel
- professional outreach
- organized tracker
- clear next steps

## Core promise
Help players present themselves professionally and stay organized throughout the recruiting process.

- START HERE Guide
- Player Resume Builder
- Player Bio Builder
- Highlight Reel Blueprint
- Coach Contact Pack
- Recruiting Tracker
- Tryout & Showcase Checklist
- Recruiting Roadmap
- Example Pack
